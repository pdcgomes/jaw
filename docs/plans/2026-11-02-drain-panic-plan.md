# jawd: a panicking conversion can't stop the inbox's drain

**The finding:** X1 of the contact cards audit (`docs/audits/2026-10-29-send-contact.md`). The converter runs `convert_rows` on a blocking thread (`sync/drain.rs:382`); a panic there comes back from `spawn_blocking` as an error, and the loop reads the same rows again without moving on or pausing. One message the converter can't take stops every later one from being converted, spins a core, and does it again at every start. The design wants a change that fails kept in quarantine, never dropped, and never blocking the changes after it ("Exactly once from at least once"), as the backfills do with `catch_unwind` (`store/write/kinds.rs`, `contacts.rs`, `copies.rs`). The history an earlier jawd kept raw has the same fault the other way (`drain.rs:483`): a panic ends its conversion for that run, at the same row each start.

## What changes

- **`drain::convert_rows`** converts each inbox row on its own, under `catch_unwind`, into a `Converted` of its own, which joins the batch's only once the row has converted. A row whose conversion panics leaves nothing of itself in the batch: no changes, no history offer, no picture, no thumbnail, no name. In its place goes `Change::Quarantined { source: "inbox", reference: <seq> }` with "converting it panicked: …", and no `Drained`, so its row stays in the inbox, raw, as one whose own conversion failed does today. The rows after it go on in the same batch, and the converter reads past it. `Converted::join` names each of the fields it takes in, so one added later that it doesn't take in fails to compile.
- **`drain::history_rows`** does the same for each row of `history_raw`: one that panics is kept in quarantine as `history_raw` `"<chat> <id>"`, the reference its `Drained::History` clears, and stays in `history_raw`.
- **`drain::inbox`** backs off when a batch's blocking task itself fails, a panic outside every row's own catch, which only the batch's bookkeeping could now make: it reads the rows again after the store error's pause, 10 ms doubling to 1 s, where it read them again at once. The history kept raw still stops for that run, as when it can't be read, and says so.
- **`write::groups`** ends a group at a `Change::Quarantined` as it does at a `Drained`: each is where a raw row's conversion ends. A row kept in quarantine is then a savepoint of its own, never rolled back with the next row's group when that one fails, nor quarantined under the next row's name.
- **`fetch::sweep`** deletes only the spool files it knows are done with (the audit's D1): a blob whose ID has its `history_kept` row, which the synced commit that drains its notification writes, and `.part` files, which the engine renames into place only once they're whole, none of them one an offer of this run names. It deleted every file no offer of this run named, and a notification kept in the inbox for a panic makes no offer, so the first sweep deleted a blob an earlier run had downloaded for it while the notification stayed. Such a blob now stays for a jawd that converts the notification, and `jawd status` counts the spool's files and their bytes (`stored.spool_files`, `stored.spool_bytes`). One new statement, a search of `history_kept`'s unique index.
- **Nothing else.** The quarantine's unique `(source, ref)` already keeps a row that fails again once, with the latest error; a `Drained` already takes a row's quarantine row with it once a later jawd converts it; and `jawd status` already counts it, in `stored.quarantined`, and its row in `stored.inbox` (or `stored.history`). No schema or interface change, and none to the protocol but the spool's two counts in `status`.
- **Left alone:** a history blob whose walk panics (`sync/fetch.rs`) fails as `Failed::Later` and waits, tried again with back-off up to a minute while the blobs after it go first; it doesn't hold the inbox up. Catching a panic per conversation inside the walk is a follow-up.

## The tests

- `sync/tests.rs`, `an_inbox_row_whose_conversion_panics_is_kept_in_quarantine_once_and_the_rows_after_it_go_on`: rows from before the converter starts (the background lane) and rows that arrive as it runs (the live lane), each with a message whose conversion panics once it has made its changes (the tests' hook, for IDs starting `3EB0PANICS`, as the backfills' tests have it; the live one's panic says only a literal, a `&str` where the other's is a `String`) among others. The rows after each are converted; nothing of the panicking message is stored; its inbox row stays; the quarantine holds one row for it, `inbox` and its seq, saying it panicked and the panic's whole message; `jawd status` counts both. Then jawd starts again on the same folder with a new row behind them: the new row is converted, and each panicking row is still in the inbox and in quarantine once. Each panicked once in each start, as the hook counts them, 200 ms after the rows after them are in, so a converter that tried one again at once fails the test.
- `sync/tests.rs`, `a_history_row_whose_conversion_panics_is_kept_in_quarantine_once_and_the_rows_after_it_go_on`: the same for `history_raw`, converted twice as two starts would, its row panicking once in each, and each pass given ten seconds to end.
- `sync/tests.rs`, `a_batch_keeps_what_each_row_converts_to_besides_its_changes_and_nothing_of_a_row_that_panics`: `convert_rows` on one batch of a notification, a picture, two rows setting your name, a link preview, a preview that panics once it has made its thumbnail and a notification that panics once it has made its offer keeps the first notification's offer, the picture's chat, the later name and the first preview's thumbnail and its row, and nothing of the two that panicked, each kept in quarantine in its place.
- `sync/tests.rs`, `a_batch_whose_conversion_panics_outside_its_rows_is_read_again_after_a_pause_that_doubles`: an inbox row whose batch panics before its rows convert, as a test hook asks, is tried 2 to 9 times in a second, and stays in the inbox.
- `sync/tests.rs`, `a_notification_whose_conversion_panics_keeps_its_blob_in_the_spool_for_a_jawd_that_converts_it`: a notification whose conversion panics once it has made its offer, its blob in the spool, then a message and a notification, beside a blob whose `history_kept` row is in and a `.part` file. With the converter and the fetcher running as jawd runs them, and what the converter hands the fetcher passing through the test, which hands over the sweep first, the converter offers only the later notification; the sweep takes the done blob and the `.part` file and keeps the panicking notification's blob, which `jawd status` counts, while that notification stays in the inbox and in quarantine. Then a jawd whose conversion of it doesn't panic walks its blob from the spool without asking the engine.
- `sync/tests.rs`, `a_sweep_that_cant_read_which_blobs_are_in_keeps_every_blob`: the spool holds a blob whose `history_kept` row is in, a blob whose row isn't and a `.part` file, and the sweep's read of `history_kept` fails, the table renamed in the test's `jaw.db`. Once a blob offered after the sweep is walked, which the fetcher does only once its sweep is done, the `.part` file has gone and both blobs are still there, as the sweep can't know which are done with.
- `store/mod.rs`, `a_row_kept_in_quarantine_is_a_step_of_its_own`: a batch of a `Change::Quarantined` and then a row whose change fails, in both lanes; both rows are in quarantine, each under its own name.

## Each rule's switch

Each is switched off in turn and its test shown failing, the results kept beside the report: no `catch_unwind` in `convert_rows`; one in `history_rows`; the row that panicked dropped from the inbox (a `Drained` for it); it not kept in quarantine; its partial changes kept (converting into the batch in place, as main does); a reference that changes at each start; and `groups` not ending at a `Change::Quarantined`. The audit's first round added: the panic's message lost, and `panicked` without its `&str` branch or its `String` one; `join` dropping each field it takes in, or keeping the earlier name; the converter not reading past a batch with a row that panicked, in the inbox and in history; the converter reading a batch that failed outside its rows again at once; and the sweep deleting every file no offer names, keeping `.part` files, keeping blobs whose rows are in, and the spool left out of `jawd status`. Its second round added the sweep deleting the blobs when it can't read which are in.

## Done when

The tests pass and each switch fails its test; `make check-generated`, `make lint`, `make build` and `make test` pass as the brief's gate says; and the database expert's audit approves the branch, `make check-audit` passing once its note and trailers are in.
