# Jaw for the Mac: typing in chats (parity step 3.1, its typing half)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Mac app shows who's typing or recording in a chat again, as jawd has said it since typing landed on 30 October (9b3fbdf4..7e047803): the typing bubble after the newest message, the header's who's typing with its counting dots, the status line's left half and the chat list's row, as [the parity plan](2026-10-27-jaw-mac-parity-plan.md)'s step 3.1 has them. Chat rows' `typing` left the protocol then (protocol 2), so every view the Mac has reads a field jawd no longer sends, and the app shows nobody typing.

**Architecture:** JawKit keeps who's typing as jaw-client's `client.rs` keeps it: a watch's or move's answer replaces it for every chat of its window, a `typing` push changes it for a chat a window shows, and it's forgotten as a chat leaves every window and when the connection drops. A frame carries all of it when it changed, and `Jawd` hands it over as `JawUpdate.typing`, after the windows' updates, which may bring in the chats it's for. The app keeps the latest, gives each pane its chat's, and gives the chat list and the status line the whole. JawUI says it in the terminal's words (`who_types` in `crates/jaw/src/view.rs`), from one place, `TypingWords`.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 (AppKit, Swift Testing), Tuist through mise, tests on the build Mac through `mac/scripts/build-mac`; jawd built here with cargo, for the live tests and `jawd demo`.

## Questions

What the design leaves open, each built as its lean, each a small change in the one place named:

1. **Names in a group.** The row said who by `id` alone, so a group said "someone's typing…"; jawd now gives each typist's `name`, as their messages name them, and `state`. A: the terminal's words, as the parity plan's first rule has them where the Mac design says nothing (lean): "typing…", or "recording…" while everyone typing is recording, and in a group the names first, "Rui typing…", "Ana, Rui typing…"; the header the same without the "…", its dots counting in its place, its names cut short to fit and its verb never. B: "someone's typing…", as before. `TypingWords`, `TypingLabel`.
2. **The status line's typing.** The Mac design has the chat list and the status line keep their green "typing…", as the terminal's is; the Mac's status line draws how the focused chat is all in grey. A: who's typing in the online colour, the rest of the line as before (lean); B: as it is. `StatusLine`.
3. **The bubble.** A: one bubble after the newest message, whoever types, its dots and colours as the owner approved them on 27 October, while the header says who (lean); B: one for each typist, in their names' colour, as the terminal draws rows. `TypingRowView`.

## Global Constraints

- **Presence isn't built here.** jawd doesn't serve it on main, where no answer carries `presence` and no `presence` push is sent; its fixtures are on `presence`, which hasn't landed. - The worktree is `~/src/jaw-mac-typing`, on `mac-typing` from main at 7e047803. Nothing is pushed; a fresh reviewer reads the branch before it lands.
- jawd is built here (`CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0 cargo build -p jawd --bin jawd`), since the build Mac has no Rust; the suites run there (`make mac-test`, `mac/scripts/build-mac build jaw`). The app runs only on `--demo`, or `--home` on a folder of this work's with a `jawd demo` it started, never on the owner's jawd. - A commit for each task, each passing `make check-parity`: task 1 takes the `typing` push's row out, as JawKit starts reading it. Commit messages as the project's: one long sentence, no prefixes.

### Task 1: JawKit keeps who's typing

- [ ] **Builds.** `Typist` (`id`, `name`, `state`, `typing` or `recording`, a state jawd may add later reading as typing) and `Typists`, who's typing by chat, oldest first; `TypingPush` (`chat`, `who`); and `typing` in `ChatsWatched` and `MessagesWatched`, each typist read on its own, so one that isn't the protocol leaves out only itself. `Incoming.typing`. The client keeps who's typing for the chats its windows show (a chat list's rows, a conversation's chat): a watch's or move's answer sets it for every chat of its window, one it leaves out having nobody typing; a push sets it for a chat a window shows, `who` empty ending it, and is dropped for one no window shows; a chat leaving every window, by a push's `remove`, an unwatch or a resync of a window being let go, is forgotten; and a dropped connection forgets everything. `Frame.typing` carries all of it when it changed, and a push wakes the caller at its next frame, as window pushes do. `Jawd.drain()` hands it over as `JawUpdate.typing`, after the windows' updates. The app takes it and does nothing yet. The `typing` push's row leaves `mac/parity.toml`.
- [ ] **Tests.** `FixtureTests`: `chats.watch.result.json`'s typing map, Rui recording in Lisbon Climbing and Ana typing in her chat, where line 78 checks only that the row's typing is empty, with the draft and mentions as before; `typing.push.json` reads as a typing push, and is among the pushes that must read as typed ones; a state jawd may add reads as typing; a typist that isn't the protocol leaves out only itself and the window still reads. `TypingClientTests`, against `FakeJawd`: a chat list's answer says who's typing in its chats, a push for one of them changes it and an empty `who` ends it, a push for a chat no window shows is dropped, and a chat the list's push removes is forgotten unless a conversation still shows it; a conversation's answer says it for its chat, one that leaves it out ends it, and a move's answer does the same for every chat of its window; unwatching forgets what only that window showed; a drop forgets it all, and the answers to watching again say how it is now; nothing changed carries no typing in the frame. `FakeJawd`'s chat rows lose `typing`, as protocol 2's have. `LiveTests`: on `jawd --engine demo`, with focus, the made-up account's people type, and the frames say Ana typing in her chat and someone recording in Lisbon Climbing.
- [ ] **Done when** JawKit's suite passes on the build Mac and `make check-parity` passes with the `typing` row out.

## Presence, afterwards
